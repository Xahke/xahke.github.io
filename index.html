Pro Football Agent

You do not manage a football club. You manage the careers of footballers — scout them, sign them, negotiate their contracts and steer them from an unknown teenager to the top of the game.

Android · Free · Turkish and English · Plays fully offline

Game details Privacy policy

About

Xahke is not a studio. It is one developer writing the code, drawing the interface and answering the support mail.

  • Offline first

    The games are built to run without a network connection. No account, no login, no server to depend on.

  • Two languages

    Turkish and English are written together, not bolted on afterwards. You can switch language at any time inside the game.

  • Answered by a person

    Support mail goes straight to the developer. There is no ticket system and no queue in between.

Support

Bug reports, questions and feedback are all welcome at xahkeee@gmail.com.

Writing in Turkish or English is equally fine. Telling us the device model and Android version usually makes a bug much faster to find.

Nothing has been released yet. Pro Football Agent is still in development and is not on Google Play. This site has no download link because there is nothing to download yet.
