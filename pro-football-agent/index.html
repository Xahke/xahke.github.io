Pro Football Agent

You do not manage a football team. You manage footballers' careers.

Find the player nobody is watching yet, convince them to sign with you, argue their wage across the table and decide which club is the right next step. Their career is your career.

What you actually do

  • Build a scouting network

    Forty-two leagues across thirty-six countries and regions — 714 clubs, from Brazil and Argentina to Japan, and twenty-eight European football nations with the big ones' second tiers. A league stays dark until you pay to open it, so where you look first matters.

  • Win clients over

    Signing a player is a conversation, not a button. Big-club names and hyped wonderkids want a reputable agent; the hidden gems at small clubs are the ones who will talk to a nobody.

  • Negotiate contracts

    Wage talks turn on real leverage: how the player has performed over their last five matches, what the club can afford, why the table was called in the first place.

  • Move them to the right club

    Transfer offers go out and come in against clubs that run their own budgets, squad limits and season-end business — not against a script written for you.

  • Grow the agency

    A skill tree in four directions — the Table, the Field, the Agency, the Network. You cannot walk all four, so an agency ends up being good at something specific.

  • Handle the weeks in between

    Media, the player, the club, the money, the crises. Events land in your inbox and each answer costs you something: cash, reputation, morale, or the client's trust.

The world keeps moving without you

Matches are simulated week by week, form and morale shift, players develop and decline, and clubs are promoted and relegated. Your clients play in more than the league: three European club cups in the current format — qualifying, a 36-club league phase, two-legged knockouts — and a national cup in each of 34 countries.

Eighty national teams call up their squads, play friendlies and qualifiers, and meet every fourth summer in a 48-team Nations Cup. The summers in between belong to the continental championships of Europe, South America, Africa, Asia and North & Central America.

Fourteen rival agencies work the same market: they sign players in the background, race you for a signature, and come after the clients you already have.

Details

Everything below reflects the current version on Google Play.
PlatformAndroid
PriceFree, with ads and optional in-app purchases
LanguagesTurkish, English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Italian — switchable in-game
ConnectionThe game is fully playable offline. No account, no login, no cloud save. Ads and purchases need a connection.
SavesThree career slots, stored on the device
ThemesFour interface themes, including the dark navy one this site is based on
AudienceIntended for players aged 13 and over
ReleaseAvailable on Google Play

On ads and purchases: the game shows ads through Google AdMob. One is a rewarded ad you start yourself, for in-game money once a day per career; the other is a full-screen ad that can open once every ten played weeks. There are no banner ads. Ads are shown only after a birth year of 18 or over has been shared, and only if Google's consent result allows them. Optional purchases through Google Play raise how many clients a career can hold, or remove the ten-week ad. The privacy policy describes all of it in detail, and it is updated before any change to ads or purchases ships.
