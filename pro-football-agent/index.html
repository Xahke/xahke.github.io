Pro Football Agent

You do not manage a football team. You manage footballers' careers.

Find the player nobody is watching yet, convince them to sign with you, argue their wage across the table and decide which club is the right next step. Their career is your career.

What you actually do Privacy policy

What you actually do

  • Build a scouting network

    Twenty-two leagues across sixteen countries and regions, from South America to Japan. A league stays dark until you pay to open it — and early on you cannot afford many, so where you look first matters.

  • Win clients over

    Signing a player is a conversation, not a button. Big-club names and hyped wonderkids want a reputable agent; the hidden gems at small clubs are the ones who will talk to a nobody.

  • Negotiate contracts

    Wage talks turn on real leverage: how the player has performed over their last five matches, what the club can afford, why the table was called in the first place.

  • Move them to the right club

    Transfer offers go out and come in against clubs that run their own budgets, squad limits and season-end business — not against a script written for you.

  • Grow the agency

    A skill tree in four directions — the Table, the Field, the Agency, the Network. You cannot walk all four, so an agency ends up being good at something specific.

  • Handle the weeks in between

    Media, the player, the club, the money, the crises. Events land in your inbox and each answer costs you something: cash, reputation, morale, or the client's trust.

The world keeps moving without you

Matches are simulated week by week, form and morale shift, players develop and decline, clubs are promoted and relegated, and every fourth season the national teams meet in a tournament.

Fourteen rival agencies work the same market: they sign players in the background, race you for a signature, and come after the clients you already have.
